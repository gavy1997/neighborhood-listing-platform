# Lab 4: Steps 2–4 Discovery & Outreach Setup

## Step 2 & 3: Selected Neutral Discovery Questions (8 Total)

1. **Workflow:** How do you currently share and update neighborhood or property information with prospective buyers or clients?
2. **Workflow:** What manual steps in your current publishing process take up the most time?
3. **Audience:** Who is your primary target audience, and how do they typically find or interact with your local content?
4. **Content Approval:** What approval steps or compliance checks must happen before a listing or local banner goes live on a community platform?
5. **Content Approval:** How do you handle content updates or corrections when information changes after publication?
6. **Success Measures:** What specific metrics or outcomes indicate that a featured neighborhood listing or local sponsorship is successful for you?
7. **Accessibility & Usability:** What accessibility standards or device preferences (mobile vs. desktop) are essential for your clients and stakeholders?
8. **General Pain Points:** What is the biggest frustration you experience with current local listing or ad management platforms?
